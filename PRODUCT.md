# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Chinese-speaking users comparing practical ways to subscribe to ChatGPT Plus, especially people without a supported overseas payment method.

## Product Purpose

Provide an original, maintainable subscription guide that earns long-tail search traffic through useful comparisons and safety guidance, then introduces AI Boxy transparently as one available self-service option.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Keyword-stuffed doorway pages, copied competitor text, fake-neutral reviews, exaggerated guarantees, and pages that hide their commercial relationship.

## Design Principles

- Answer the search question before presenting the brand.
- Compare options with visible tradeoffs and safety boundaries.
- Keep AI Boxy recommendations explicit and attributable.
- Prefer a small number of maintainable pages over scaled thin content.
- Make every purchase link measurable with stable UTM parameters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, strong contrast, keyboard-visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
